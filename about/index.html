Research and publications

My research background connects distributed computing and applied machine learning with earlier work on educational software and connected-device security:

  • Streaming data pipelines: my 2019 CSUF thesis, Git-Archive Data Pipeline, explores scalable pipelines and streaming graph processing with Kafka and Flink.
  • Applied machine learning: I coauthored Prediction of Crop Cultivation for IEEE CCWC 2019, comparing regression approaches for crop-yield prediction.
  • Distributed analytics: I am the first-listed author of the CCWC 2019 paper on Hadoop and Apache Mahout.
  • Educational software: my 2016 article Online Examination Empowerment describes a Java-based examination-preparation framework developed at Ganpat University.
  • IoT security: my 2016 survey article examines connected-device applications and security concerns.

The Publications collection preserves complete author lists, venues, source links, and citations. These published records are distinct from my current research interests and exploratory workflows.
